## Not sure of
- **OIR / OIA / OIDO**: Three forms of the same verb in one grid (35D, 10D, 39A). OIDO is clued as the noun (the sense of hearing), but an editor would reject this. Every floor-50 fill of this skeleton had OIDO and OIR; OIA is avoidable with more curation.
- **OSO / OSA**: Bear and she-bear in the same grid (30A, 23A). OSA is clued as the constellation.
- **ALEGRO**: Clued as the tempo (RAE accepts 'alegro' for Italian 'allegro'; many musicians write 'allegro'). The other reading is 'alegró' (cheered up).
- **Conjugations**: Too many for one puzzle: REI (reí), ORE (ore), ANDE, NADE, CEDO, ACUDO, ARDO, VAS, OIA, EMITE, PROHIBA, CAVAMOS, APODERO (apoderó), ARRASO (arrasó), ERASE (érase). REI, ORE and ARDO are the least natural.
- **APODERO**: Rare verb (apoderar: to grant power of attorney). Weakest long entry.
- **FACES**: Plural of 'faz'; literary.
- **RECATO / SESO / IBIS / CRIOLLA**: Real words, lower frequency. CRIOLLA is clued through Peruvian 'música criolla'.
- **ARRASO**: 'Arrasar' also means to devastate; clued in its cheerful sense (to sweep the awards).
- **ENARMONIA**: The pun is thin: 'en armonía' is nearly literal. It is the only 9-letter music idiom that fitted the centre; A UNÍSONO (ALUNISONO) was the alternative.
- **DARELDODEPECHO**: Idiom is common in Spain; understood but less used in parts of Latin America.
- **Blocks**: 42 black squares (18.7%) is on the high side for a 15×15; the usual ceiling is about 38.

## How it was built
- Wanted five themers (14/11/9/11/14 with TOCAR DE OÍDO and SIN TON NI SON). findSkeleton returned no skeleton for five or four themers at floors 47 and up in dozens of seeds; three themers (14/9/14) work.
- First skeleton (38 blocks): unfillable at floor 50, and at 47 every fill had 6 to 18 entries below 50 with forced junk around the centre (ORIOLES, INFLA, LIRON, VOSA). Abandoned after five curation rounds (160 to 500 fills each).
- Second search: 12 finder runs (six seeds, centre ENARMONIA or ALUNISONO, 45 s budget each). Four skeletons filled at floor 50; this one (seed 16, 42 blocks, 74 entries) was the cleanest.
- On this skeleton: 400 fills at floor 50 gave 390 distinct grids, none with an engine entry below 50. With the curation dislikes all excluded the skeleton is unfillable at 50, so some mild ones were let back in.
- Every one of the 390 fills contains OVAL or ORAL, which the midi and the Plus first used. Rejecting them here made all 260 tries time out (3 s each), so the midi and Plus corners were changed instead.
- A hand-designed 34-block skeleton failed on a four-letter down that had to end in V.
