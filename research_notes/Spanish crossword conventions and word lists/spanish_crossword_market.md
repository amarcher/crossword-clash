# Market for daily digital crosswords in Spanish (state as of November 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-13. Method note for the report writer: about 29 search/fetch calls. Several primary pages could not be read (elpais.com is blocked to the fetch tool; Google Play listings came back truncated), so a good part of the publisher picture rests on trade press and third-party round-ups, some of them old (2021–2023). Every such case is flagged. Where I found nothing, the item is in Gaps rather than guessed.

## 1. Which newspapers and media offer a daily digital crossword in Spanish (format, paywall, in-house vs syndicated)?

### Takeaway
Daily Spanish crosswords exist at the big Spanish and Argentine dailies (El País, La Vanguardia, ABC/Vocento, El Debate, elDiario.es, La Nación, Los Andes, Emol/El Mercurio), almost always as a classic Spanish-style crucigrama plus, at several, a "mini", delivered through white-label platforms (Amuse Labs/PuzzleMe, Arkadium) and mostly free to play, with paywalls reserved for extras (ranking, history). I found no evidence of a Spanish daily crossword at the US Hispanic outlets named (Univision, Telemundo, La Opinión, El Nuevo Herald, NYT en Español); the only US-publisher Spanish offering found is the Washington Post's Arkadium-supplied Spanish games pages.

**Syndication and platforms**
- Amuse Labs says PuzzleMe supports crossword creation in Spanish and 35 other languages, inserts locale characters on the mobile keyboard automatically, and can connect publishers with professional constructors in Spain among other countries. — [Amuse Labs FAQ](https://amuselabs.com/faqs/); [Amuse Labs publishers](https://amuselabs.com/solutions/publishers/)
- Arkadium reports, for its white-label game sections, 15.2 minutes average time on site, 6.8 page views per session and a bounce rate under 15% versus about 55% industry average (figures as cited in Dec 2021). — [Laboratorio de Periodismo](https://laboratoriodeperiodismo.org/los-periodicos-retoman-las-apuestas-por-las-secciones-de-juegos-y-pasatiempos/)
- Keesing owns the main Spanish print puzzle brands (Quiz, Megastar) and runs "Mis Pasatiempos", an online store launched in 2022. — [Keesing](https://www.keesing.com/spanish-team-enjoys-success-with-popular-tv-quiz-shows)

### Inferences
- The Spanish newspaper crossword is a commodity retention feature on rented platforms, not a flagship product: named setters exist (Tarkus, Mambrino's archive) but no outlet found markets a crossword brand the way NYT does, and several run archive or recycled puzzles (El País's "365 selected by Tarkus", Mambrino's digitised back catalogue).
- A "mini" already exists at El País, El Debate and nacion.com, so the mini format alone is not novel in Spanish. Whether those minis are fully crossed is unverified.
- The US Hispanic market looks the emptiest of the three regions for publisher crosswords.

### Gaps

- Which platform supplies La Nación (AR), El Debate, Emol, Los Andes: not found.
- Grid style (fully crossed vs Spanish-style with unchecked letters) of each publisher's daily and mini: not verified by inspection.
- No source found for Puzzle Society or a Spanish puzzle syndicate supplying digital dailies.

## 2. Which mobile apps lead for Spanish crosswords (downloads, ratings, publishers, monetisation, reviews)?

### Takeaway
The app market is populated by free, ad-supported, offline puzzle packs from small studios (Litera Games, FgCos, Quarzo Apps, MBEX, Educoelho) and by the global CodyCross; the leading titles are autodefinido or classic-grid packs with no daily editorial puzzle. I could not retrieve current download counts from the stores.

### Cited Findings
- "Crucigrama — Autodefinido" (MBEX LTD EOOD): 4.7/5 from about 2,500 ratings on the Spanish App Store, No. 16 in the free Trivia chart in Spain (read 2026-11-13), free, no subscription mentioned; autodefinido format (clues inside cells, "Scandinavian-inspired"), 45,000+ words and 5,489 puzzles, offline, unlimited hints, large letters, no time limits. — [App Store ES](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/id1627932335)
- Reviews shown for that app praise variety; one user reports having finished all the puzzles and the developer replies more are coming (sample of five reviews only). — [App Store ES](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/id1627932335)
- "Crucigramas en español + Juego de vocabulario" was described as the most popular free crossword app on Google Play with over a million downloads (article undated in my results, likely 2020–2022). — [Androidphoria](https://androidphoria.com/aplicaciones/crucigramas)
- CodyCross is listed among the most-downloaded crossword apps on both stores for Spanish users; it is a themed word game, not a newspaper-style grid. — [tuapppara](https://tuapppara.com/juegos/android/crucigramas/); [App Store MX](https://apps.apple.com/MX/app/id1092689152)
- "Crucigramas - en español" (FgCos Games): free, released Aug 2017, last updated July 2024, no time limit. — [Skich listing](https://skich.app/pt-br/games/crucigramas-en-espaol-3)
- "Crucigrama en español" (Litera Games): offline play; AppGoblin's keyword analysis for the term shows a median of 7,135 installs and a mean rating of 4.0 across competing apps. — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.litera.games.crosswords.spanish/keywords/discovery)
- Other titles surfacing in searches: Crucigramas Pasatiempos, "Crucigramas en Español :)" (Educoelho, free), Ciudad de Palabras (word-connect), Quarzo Apps' classic crosswords and generator. — [App Store MX](https://apps.apple.com/MX/app/id1635795449); [tuapppara](https://tuapppara.com/juegos/android/crucigramas/)

### Inferences
- The category is monetised by ads, not subscriptions; I found no Spanish crossword app selling a subscription.
- Content is static packs, sometimes exhaustible (the "finished them all" review), with no dated daily, author bylines, themes or current references.
- An iOS rating count of ~2,500 for a top-20 Trivia app in Spain suggests modest absolute scale for dedicated crossword apps there (estimate; iOS is the minority platform in Spain and Latin America).

### Gaps
- Google Play install bands, ratings and review themes for Litera, FgCos and others: pages returned truncated. Needs a manual check or an app-intelligence tool (Sensor Tower, AppMagic, data.ai).
- No systematic review mining was possible; complaints (ads, obscure words, regional vocabulary) are therefore unverified.
- Latin American store charts (MX, AR, CO) not checked.

## 3. Are there Spanish crossword products with multiplayer, leaderboards, streaks or social features?

### Takeaway
Only thin versions exist: El País advertised a "multiplayer mode"/invite link in 2021 (a PuzzleMe co-solve feature), and La Nación has a subscriber-only ranking. I found no Spanish-language real-time crossword race, friends system, or NYT-style streak-and-leaderboard product.

### Cited Findings
- El País's 2021 relaunch listed "multiplayer mode and the ability to pause and save" as new features, without detail. — [Red de Periodistas](https://www.reddeperiodistas.com/la-web-de-el-pais-relanza-su-seccion-de-crucigramas-y-juegos/)
- A round-up notes El País's crossword "has a function to generate a link to invite someone to play with you". — [internetpasoapaso](https://internetpasoapaso.com/mejores-paginas-webs-crucigramas/)
- La Nación: ranking and calendar history are subscriber-only; the FAQ mentions no streaks; a ranking page exists. — [La Nación FAQ, 17 Jun 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/juegos/ln-juegos-preguntas-frecuentes-nid17062026/); [LN ranking](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/juegos/ranking/)
- El Debate's crossword page describes no ranking or streak. — [El Debate](https://www.eldebate.com/juegos/crucigrama/)
- App search for multiplayer Spanish crosswords surfaced "Crossword Master - Crucigramas" (turn-based word-building on a crossword board against an opponent, free with IAP) and generic "play with friends" claims in translated global apps; none is a shared-grid race on an editorial daily, and no result mentioned a leaderboard. — [App Store MX](https://apps.apple.com/MX/app/id6547858927)

### Inferences
- Competitive, social crossword play in Spanish appears to be unoccupied; the closest are a collaborative link (El País) and a paywalled points table (La Nación).

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether El País's multiplayer still exists in 2026 or how it works.
- Apalabrados/Scrabble-like Spanish social word games (a proxy for social word-game appetite) were not researched.

## 4. Audience size signals

### Takeaway
The addressable language population is very large (about 636 million potential Spanish users worldwide in 2025; roughly 40–45 million people speaking Spanish at home in the US), and Spanish Wordle's 2022 take-off shows word-game demand, heavily concentrated in Spain. Hard numbers on Spanish crossword audiences (publisher games traffic, search volume by country, magazine circulation) were not found.

### Cited Findings
- Instituto Cervantes yearbook 2025 (presented 28 Oct 2025): 635.7 million Spanish speakers/potential users worldwide, about 30 million (5%) more than the previous year; native speakers above 500 million (one report: 520 million). — [La Nación, 28 Oct 2025](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/cultura/mas-de-635-millones-de-personas-hablan-espanol-crece-5-en-un-ano-y-se-afianza-como-la-tercera-lengua-nid28102025/); [Euronews, 28 Oct 2025](https://es.euronews.com/2025/10/28/el-espanol-suma-30-millones-de-hablantes-y-alcanza-los-635-millones-en-el-mundo)
- US: around 60 million Spanish speakers by press accounts of the Cervantes data, 57+ million in a Bankinter post citing 2024 data (these include limited-competence speakers). — [Bankinter](https://www.bankinter.com/blog/economia/paises-hispanohablantes-mundo)
- US Census-based: about 44.9 million people aged 5+ speak Spanish at home, 1 in 7. — [USAFacts](https://usafacts.org/answers/how-many-people-speak-spanish-at-home/country/united-states/)
- Pew: US Latino population rose from 35.3 million (2000) to 68 million (2024). — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/category/race-ethnicity/racial-ethnic-groups/hispanics-latinos/hispanic-latino-demographics/hispanics-latinos-and-language/)
- Share of US Latinos speaking Spanish at home fell from 78% (2000) to 68% (2024), per secondary reports of Pew data (not checked against Pew's page). — [Voz](https://voz.us/en/society/261006/39682/demographic-paradox-or-successful-assimilation-why-fewer-hispanics-speak-spanish-at-home-despite-the-language-s-nationwide-boom.html)
- Spanish Wordle (built by Daniel Rodríguez, a Colombian engineer in Austin, Jan 2022): about 1,000 users on day one, 20,000 on day two, 40,000 on day three, 52,000 by the following Sunday, mostly in Spain. — [Xataka](https://www.xataka.com/videojuegos/habla-daniel-rodriguez-creador-wordle-espanol-asi-adapto-dos-tardes-juego-online-moda)
- A later report put Spain at 88% of Spanish Wordle users, Argentina a distant second (Jan 2022). — [Los Andes](https://www.losandes.com.ar/muy-tecno/wordle-el-juego-online-que-se-volvio-viral-y-rompio-las-reglas-de-internet)
- elDiario.es, a year on, called Wordle "the crossword of the 21st century" played by millions (headline framing; no Spanish-version figure extracted). — [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/tecnologia/ano-fenomeno-wordle-juego-viral-crucigrama-siglo-xxi-millones-personas_1_9844449.html)
- Catalan precedent for a native-language word-game hit: Paraulògic (a Spelling Bee analogue) is discussed alongside Wordle as a phenomenon. — [Ara](https://es.ara.cat/estilo/fenomeno-paraulogic-wordle-gustan-juegos-linguisticos_1_4307344.amp.html)
- El País: 497,000 subscribers at end of Sept 2026 (489,000 digital), up 14% year on year; the split between Spain and the Americas is not published. The article's headline says "exceeds 500,000". — [Laboratorio de Periodismo, 9 Nov 2026](https://laboratoriodeperiodismo.org/el-pais-supera-los-500-000-suscriptores-tras-aumentar-un-14-su-base-de-abonados-en-el-ultimo-ano/)
- Clarín is described as the Spanish-language newspaper with the most digital subscribers in the world. — [LatAm Journalism Review](https://latamjournalismreview.org/articles/7-lessons-on-how-argentinas-clarin-became-the-spanish-language-newspaper-with-the-most-digital-subscribers-in-the-world/)
- Print puzzles in Spain: Quiz (since 1973) is the best-known puzzle-magazine brand, sold through 9,000 points of distribution; Megastar (since 2012; Pasapalabra and Telecinco titles) through 13,000. Printed puzzle sales rose more than 30% during the 2020 lockdown, according to Keesing Spain's David Wakefield. — [Keesing](https://www.keesing.com/spanish-team-enjoys-success-with-popular-tv-quiz-shows); [Red de Periodistas](https://www.reddeperiodistas.com/la-web-de-el-pais-relanza-su-seccion-de-crucigramas-y-juegos/)
- Search-demand proxy: crossword-clue solver sites rank for "X crucigrama" queries with roughly 1,000–1,400 monthly searches each in Colombia (Semrush overview of fsolver.es, Oct 2025). — [Semrush](https://es.semrush.com/website/fsolver.es/overview)
- The Spanish crossword turned 100 in 2025: first published 22 March 1925 in Blanco y Negro. — [elDiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/cultura/elogio-crucigrama_1_12194868.html)

### Inferences
- Spain is the most engaged market per head for Spanish word games (88% of early Spanish Wordle); Latin America is larger by population but showed weaker early uptake; that Wordle split is from Jan 2022 and may reflect how the game spread, not durable demand.
- The US at-home Spanish population (40–45 million) is comparable to Spain's whole population, but the home-use share is declining and many are bilingual, so a Spanish daily there competes with English products.
- The existence of clue-solver sites with steady traffic indicates an active habitual solver base in Latin America (Colombia at least).

### Gaps
- Google Trends or keyword volumes for "crucigrama", "crucigramas", "crucigrama diario" vs "autodefinido" by country: not obtained (Trends is not readable by the tools used). Hypothesis to test, unverified: "autodefinido" is a Spain-specific term and "crucigrama" dominates in Latin America.
- No published traffic, player or play-count figures for El País Juegos, LN Juegos or any Spanish-language publisher's games.
- No data found on puzzle or word-game engagement specifically among US Hispanic audiences.
- No circulation (OJD) figures for Spanish puzzle magazines; none for Latin American ones (e.g. Argentina's Quijote).
- Current (2026) usage of Spanish Wordle variants: not found.

## 5. Willingness to pay

### Takeaway
Nobody found sells a standalone Spanish puzzle subscription. Payment for Spanish puzzles happens in two ways: bundled into news subscriptions (El País's subscriber-only game functions, La Nación's ranking and history, nacion.com's Premium catalogue) and print (puzzle magazines and El País Juegos books). No figures exist publicly on games-driven subscriptions at Spanish-language publishers.

### Cited Findings
- La Nación (AR) gates ranking and history to subscribers while play stays free. — [La Nación FAQ, 17 Jun 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/juegos/ln-juegos-preguntas-frecuentes-nid17062026/)
- nacion.com (Costa Rica) sells its nine-game catalogue as a Premium benefit at 12.99 a month (currency not confirmed). — [nacion.com](https://www.nacion.com/suscripcion-digital-premium/)
- El País counts its games section and Conexiones among the product features supporting a base of 497,000 subscribers (Sept 2026), with no games-specific numbers disclosed. — [Laboratorio de Periodismo](https://laboratoriodeperiodismo.org/el-pais-supera-los-500-000-suscriptores-tras-aumentar-un-14-su-base-de-abonados-en-el-ultimo-ano/)
- The 2021 trade view in Spain: games, puzzles and cooking sections are opened free to build habit but "in the long run will sit behind the paywall", valued for high dwell time and recurrence. — [Laboratorio de Periodismo, 29 Dec 2021](https://laboratoriodeperiodismo.org/los-periodicos-retoman-las-apuestas-por-las-secciones-de-juegos-y-pasatiempos/)
- English-language benchmark: more than a million subscribers pay specifically for NYT Games (as reported by Digiday's Spanish edition; date of figure not captured, and the number is older than 2026). — [Digiday ES](https://digiday.com/es/por-que-el-new-york-times-establece-conexiones-con-jugadores-de-videojuegos-para-diversificar-su-audiencia/)
- An NYT games executive says users who engage weekly with both news and games show the best subscription retention (IJNet, Aug 2024, Spanish-language piece on strategies for publishers). — [IJNet](https://ijnet.org/es/story/estrategias-para-que-los-medios-incrementen-sus-suscriptores-con-juegos-en-linea)
- Print willingness to pay: Keesing's Spanish store sells magazine subscriptions; Diez Minutos sells a 100-page puzzle special for 1 euro on top of the magazine price (spring 2026). — [Keesing](https://www.keesing.com/spanish-team-enjoys-success-with-popular-tv-quiz-shows); [Diez Minutos](https://www.diezminutos.es/estilo-de-vida/ocio/a70831412/diez-minutos-pasatiempos-primavera-2026/)
- The leading Spanish crossword apps found are free; the Catalan Màrius Serra app uses in-app purchases. — [App Store ES](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/id1627932335); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/mots-encreuats-de-m%C3%A0rius-serra/id1016454983)

### Inferences
- Spanish-speaking solvers are used to free digital puzzles; paying is proven only for print and for bundles. A standalone paid Spanish puzzle tier is untested, which is both an opening and a risk.
- Publishers gate status features (ranking, history) rather than puzzles, which suggests those are what they believe converts.
- Purchasing power differs sharply: a US-dollar subscription price that works in the US and Spain is likely too high for much of Latin America (inference; no pricing data gathered).

### Gaps
- No conversion, retention or revenue figures tied to games at El País, La Nación, Clarín or Vocento.
- No evidence either way on a standalone Spanish-language puzzle subscription anywhere.
- App-store revenue estimates for Spanish crossword apps not obtained.

## 6. Gaps in the market: what does nobody offer?

### Takeaway
On the evidence found, nobody offers in Spanish: a dedicated daily-crossword brand with original, bylined, themed puzzles; a real-time multiplayer race; a friends graph with leaderboards and streaks open to non-subscribers; or a native app built around a daily. The US Hispanic audience has almost no publisher offering at all. These conclusions are "not found", not "proven absent".

### Cited Findings
- Publisher crosswords are white-label modules inside news sites (Amuse Labs, Arkadium), with archive-based content at the market leader (365 puzzles selected by Tarkus; the late Mambrino's digitised archive). — [Red de Periodistas](https://www.reddeperiodistas.com/la-web-de-el-pais-relanza-su-seccion-de-crucigramas-y-juegos/)
- Social features found are limited to an invite link/multiplayer mode at El País (2021) and a subscriber-only ranking at La Nación (2026); La Nación has no save-progress and no dedicated app. — [internetpasoapaso](https://internetpasoapaso.com/mejores-paginas-webs-crucigramas/); [La Nación FAQ](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/juegos/ln-juegos-preguntas-frecuentes-nid17062026/)
- Apps are static offline packs monetised by ads, in autodefinido or classic style, without a dated daily. — [App Store ES](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/id1627932335); [Skich](https://skich.app/pt-br/games/crucigramas-en-espaol-3)
- Minis exist at El País, El Debate and nacion.com. — [Red de Periodistas](https://www.reddeperiodistas.com/la-web-de-el-pais-relanza-su-seccion-de-crucigramas-y-juegos/); [El Debate](https://www.eldebate.com/juegos/mini-crucigrama/2026-09-30/); [nacion.com](https://www.nacion.com/suscripcion-digital-premium/)
- A themed crossword exists at La Nación ("Cruci temático"). — [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/juegos/cruci-tematico/)
- In the US, the only Spanish crossword from a major publisher found is the Washington Post's Arkadium pages. — [Washington Post](https://games.washingtonpost.com/es/juegos/crucigramas-faciles)

### Inferences
- Likely open ground, in descending confidence: (1) real-time races and friends leaderboards on a Spanish daily; (2) a standalone app with a dated daily, streaks and archive; (3) US Spanish speakers and pan-regional (neutral-vocabulary) puzzles; (4) American-style fully crossed 15×15 themed puzzles in Spanish, which I did not see anywhere but could not rule out, and which may be unfamiliar rather than missed: Spanish solvers are raised on grids with unchecked letters and on autodefinidos.
- Not gaps: a mini and a themed crossword already exist at publishers, so differentiation would have to come from quality, social play and product, not the formats alone.
- Regional vocabulary is a real product question: early Spanish Wordle was 88% Spain, and puzzles set by Spain-based constructors (El País, ABC) are the norm; a Latin American or US solver may be poorly served by peninsular references (inference).

### Gaps
- Whether any Spanish outlet publishes a fully crossed American-style grid: not verified; needs inspection of actual puzzles at El País mini, El Debate mini, LN Cruci.
- Solver preference between crucigrama and autodefinido by country: no data.
- No user research found on what Spanish-speaking solvers say is missing.
