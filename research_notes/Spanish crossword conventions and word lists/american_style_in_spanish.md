# American-style (fully crossed) crosswords in Spanish

Research date: 2026-11-13. Overall caveat for the report writer: public, citable material on this topic is thin. I found a handful of outlets and indie constructors, almost no first-person constructor commentary on filling fully crossed Spanish grids, and no published scored Spanish word list for crossword software. Several primary pages (elpais.com, crosshare.org) refused automated fetches, so some items rest on search-result snippets and are marked as such.

## 1. Which outlets, apps and constructors publish fully crossed American-style crosswords or minis in Spanish?

### Takeaway
A few Spanish outlets run a daily "mini" (El País since July 2021, El Debate through 2026) and one puzzle site sells a product explicitly named "Crucigrama Americano", but I could not confirm from primary pages that any of them is strictly fully crossed. The clearest self-described American-style work in Spanish is indie, on Crosshare (constructor "elro"); no major US outlet (NYT included) was found publishing a Spanish-language crossword.

### Cited Findings
- El País announced a new pasatiempos section in July 2021 listing "Crucigramas mini + actualizaciones diarias + archivo de Mambrino y Tarkus + Sudokus con cinco niveles de dificultad + sopa de letras temática". — [EL PAÍS on X](https://x.com/el_pais/status/1413482349698994176)
- Third-party reviews (2026 list) describe El País's games section as offering a Sunday crossword, an easy crossword and a mini crucigrama. — [internetpasoapaso](https://internetpasoapaso.com/mejores-paginas-webs-crucigramas/)
- El Debate (Spain) publishes a daily "Crucigrama mini" with dated pages through 2026 (e.g. 7 July 2026, 30 Sept 2026). Its page describes "una cuadrícula de casillas en blanco y negro", says "Las casillas negras sirven como separadores entre las palabras", has 9 clues split into Horizontales and Verticales, and names no constructor. — [El Debate mini](https://www.eldebate.com/juegos/mini-crucigrama/); [dated example](https://www.eldebate.com/juegos/mini-crucigrama/2026-07-07/)
- PasatiemposWeb publishes a product called "Crucigrama Americano", stating "diseñamos Crucigramas Americanos adaptados al público hispanohablante", in three levels (Fácil, Medio, Difícil). It describes the format as having rotational symmetry, far fewer black squares than the traditional crucigrama, and every letter belonging to an across and a down word ("permite verificar las respuestas por doble vía"), "habitualmente de 15x15 o incluso 21x21 casillas". — [PasatiemposWeb](https://www.pasatiemposweb.com/crucigrama_americano.php)
- Crosshare constructor elro (@elromancin): profile describes mostly American-style crosswords in Spanish, over 20 published, including a series of "Español-dle" puzzles, one titled "UN BLOQUE NEGRO EN MI ESPAÑOLDLE 5X5". (From search snippet; the profile page returned 403 to direct fetch.) — [Crosshare elro](https://crosshare.org/elromancin)
- Crosshare has a Spanish interface (crosshare.org/es/) and Spanish-tagged minis, e.g. "Jueves, 28 de Abril de 2022 (Menú español.)" tagged lang-es, mini. — [Crosshare puzzle](https://crosshare.org/crosswords/GW3SO9COAM5ICKNkim9L/jueves-28-de-abril-de-2022-menu-espanol)
- "Cruci-Cross" on Crosshare is a bilingual mini: across answers in Spanish without accent marks, downs in English. — [Crosshare Cruci-Cross](https://crosshare.org/crosswords/3V66quRRU85HOsUnB4Rn/cruci-cross)
- Bryan Betancur made "Bilingual Puzzle #1" (hosted on Crossword Hobbyist), with Spanish words as significant entries and many clues in Spanish; covered 16 Aug 2018 by PuzzleNation/PuzzCulture, whose reviewer called it a "confusing solve" because it was unclear whether an answer would be English or Spanish. — [PuzzCulture](https://puzzculture.com/tag/bilingual); [puzzle](https://crosswordhobbyist.com/510715)
- LA Times Sunday crossword of 22 Sept 2024 by Zhouqin Burnikel had a "Spanglish" theme: common phrases with one English word swapped for a Spanish one (an English puzzle, not a Spanish one). — [LAXCrossword](https://laxcrossword.com/2024/09/22/)
- The New York Times: no Spanish-language crossword or mini found. NYT en Español (news) launched Feb 2016 and was shut in Sept 2019 for financial reasons; coverage mentions no puzzles. NYT Wordplay ran an explainer on Spanish words common in its English puzzles (Feb 2018). — [Nieman Lab 2019](https://www.niemanlab.org/2019/09/the-new-york-times-shutters-nyt-en-espanol-after-three-years-it-did-not-prove-financially-successful/); [NYT Wordplay](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/02/15/crosswords/10-spanish-words.html)
- The Washington Post games site has Spanish-language pages for "Crucigramas Fáciles" and "Crucigramas Difíciles" (URLs surfaced in search; grid style not inspected). — [WaPo fáciles](https://games.washingtonpost.com/es/juegos/crucigramas-faciles)
- Alejandro Cernuda (aCernuda.com) publishes automatically generated 15x15 Spanish crosswords, archive 2024 to 2026 ("Crucigramas de alta calidad generados automáticamente"); whether fully crossed is not stated. — [aCernuda](https://crucigrama.acernuda.com/)
- A 2013 Spanish-language cryptic was made by a non-native English-speaking blogger (DA Trippers), who per search results later helped translate Crosshare into Spanish; a British-style, not American-style, experiment. — [DA Trippers](https://dacryptic.wordpress.com/2013/06/02/una-novedad-un-crucigrama-criptico-en-castellano/)
- La Nación (Argentina) has an active games section with crossword how-to articles (2024). — [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/juegos/trucos-y-consejos/como-resolver-un-crucigrama-nivel-facil-intermedio-y-dificil-nid10072024/)

### Inferences
- El Debate's mini has 9 clues; an open 5x5 has 10, so the grid probably contains at least one black square or is smaller than 5x5. This is arithmetic, not confirmed by viewing the grid.
- The fully crossed Spanish format exists mainly as (a) newspaper minis, where full crossing is easy at 5x5, and (b) hobbyist output on Crosshare. I found no evidence of an established Spanish-language daily 15x15 in NYT style from a major publisher.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm grid rules of El País's mini (elpais.com blocked fetches) or who constructs it.
- Found nothing on Clarín, Infobae, La Vanguardia, Univision, Telemundo, Duolingo, Apple News+, LinkedIn, Andrews McMeel/Puzzle Society, The New Yorker or AVCX publishing Spanish American-style puzzles. Absence in my searches is not proof of absence; these were not each checked on their own sites.
- Whether WaPo's Spanish crosswords (likely a syndicated provider) are fully crossed is unverified.

## 2. Grid sizes and American rules: kept or relaxed?

### Takeaway
Sources that describe an "americano" in Spanish state the same rules as in English (rotational symmetry, every letter checked); minis are around 5x5 and full-size is described as 15x15. Evidence on where real Spanish puzzles relax the rules is indirect.

### Cited Findings
- PasatiemposWeb's Spanish "Crucigrama Americano": rotational symmetry, every letter in an across and a down word; minimum word length not mentioned. — [PasatiemposWeb](https://www.pasatiemposweb.com/crucigrama_americano.php)
- A Spanish KDP tool vendor says that in Spain "conviven el crucigrama tipo americano (cuadrícula simétrica con cuadros negros) y el tipo francés", and that Spanish crosswords typically use dictionary definitions, longer words and more black squares (promotional source, search snippet only). — [KDPEasy](https://www.kdpeasy.com/es/features/generador-crucigramas)
- Spanish Wikipedia notes a traditional Spanish convention foreign to the American format: answers may be entered reversed when crossings do not fit, flagged "al revés" in the clue; it also lists the "silábico" variant (one syllable per cell) and "crucigrama blanco". — [Wikipedia ES](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crucigrama)
- General statement of American conventions used as the benchmark: 180-degree symmetry, no one- or two-letter entries, complete interlock. — [puzzle-generator.com](https://puzzle-generator.com/en/american-crosswords.html)
- elro's Crosshare titles include a 5x5 with one black square ("UN BLOQUE NEGRO EN MI ESPAÑOLDLE 5X5"). — [Crosshare elro](https://crosshare.org/elromancin)

### Inferences
- Reversed entries, two-letter words and unchecked squares are the traditional Spanish escape valves; an American-format Spanish puzzle gives all three up at once, which is where the difficulty concentrates.

### Gaps
- No source gave block counts or word counts for Spanish American-style 15x15 grids.
- Could not inspect actual grids from El País, El Debate or PasatiemposWeb to verify stated rules.

## 3. Ñ, accents, diéresis, digraphs

### Takeaway
Little is documented. The one concrete data point is a bilingual Crosshare mini that enters Spanish answers without accent marks; tools differ on whether Ñ survives.

### Cited Findings
- Cruci-Cross enters Spanish across answers "without accent marks". — [Crosshare](https://crosshare.org/crosswords/3V66quRRU85HOsUnB4Rn/cruci-cross)
- KDPEasy claims many English-language generators turn "España" into "ESPANA", producing visible errors, and advertises support for tildes and ñ (promotional). — [KDPEasy](https://www.kdpeasy.com/es/features/generador-crucigramas)
- Crossword Compiler: each word list has a letter mapping defining equivalent letters; "Set all accents equivalent" maps accented letters to unaccented ones, so keeping Ñ distinct while folding Á/É/Í/Ó/Ú is a mapping choice. Lists can use full Unicode or restricted character sets. — [Crossword Compiler help](https://crossword-compiler.com/en/help/html/equivalentlettersinwordlists.htm)
- For comparison, German convention writes Ä/Ö/Ü as AE/OE/UE and ß as SS in grids. — [puzzle-generator.com](https://puzzle-generator.com/en/american-crosswords.html)

### Inferences
- The workable convention (accents and diéresis dropped, Ñ a distinct letter, CH/LL as two cells) is standard practice in Spanish crucigramas by general knowledge, but none of the pages I could read states it explicitly.

### Gaps
- No outlet's stated policy on Ñ, Ü, CH, LL or RR was found. El País and El Debate pages do not address it.

## 4. What constructors say about difficulty of filling fully crossed Spanish grids

### Takeaway
I found no first-person account from a Spanish-language constructor on fill difficulty, word-length distribution, vowel endings or conjugations as fill. This is the largest gap in the research.

### Cited Findings
- A generator vendor argues English suits the American format because of its many short words, that languages with few three-letter words (German cited) need large mixed lists with short entries kept, and that "a few hundred words is tight for a 15×15" while tens of thousands regularly reach 100% interlock (vendor claims, not about Spanish specifically). — [puzzle-generator.com](https://puzzle-generator.com/en/american-crosswords.html)
- In English, full checking already forces constructors onto abbreviations, names, partials and foreign words. — [Always Puzzling](https://alwayspuzzling.blogspot.com/2013/01/american-vs-british-crosswords.html)
- The non-native author of the 2013 Spanish cryptic reported the hard part was not the grid (software handled it) but the lack of a Spanish reference for crossword abbreviations and conventions. — [DA Trippers](https://dacryptic.wordpress.com/2013/06/02/una-novedad-un-crucigrama-criptico-en-castellano/)

### Inferences
- That fully crossed Spanish output found is overwhelmingly mini-sized is consistent with 15x15 fill being hard, but it could equally reflect market habit.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, forum, podcast or blog discussion of Spanish fill statistics surfaced. No source on practical block counts for Spanish 15x15s.

## 5. Software and word lists

### Takeaway
General-purpose tools support Spanish (Crossword Compiler, PuzzleMe, GNOME Crosswords via hunspell), but I found no published scored Spanish word list in the `WORD;SCORE` format used by CrossFire, Ingrid or the Crossword Nexus list. Raw Spanish word lists are plentiful and unscored.

### Cited Findings
- Crossword Compiler's optional lists include Wikipedia-derived word lists in French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Spanish. — [cosmologist.info word lists](https://new.cosmologist.info/wordlists.html)
- Amuse Labs' PuzzleMe offers "Spanish (Spain)" as a game language among 36+ languages (article 13 June 2024). — [Amuse Labs](https://amuselabs.com/?p=9616)
- GNOME Crosswords (Jonathan Blandford) issue #39 proposes building lists from hunspell dictionaries: `es.dic` 70,159 lines, `es_ES.dic` 57,086, `es_MX.dic` 57,997; notes they lack priority scores like Peter Broda's list and need affix expansion to get inflected forms. — [GNOME Crosswords #39](https://gitlab.gnome.org/jrb/crosswords/-/issues/39)
- Unscored Spanish lists: `an-array-of-spanish-words` (~636,000 words, from the Letterpress list) — [GitHub](https://github.com/words/an-array-of-spanish-words); `davidlorenzana/spanish_lexicon` (based on an RAE dump of 2022-05-01) — [GitHub](https://github.com/davidlorenzana/spanish_lexicon); `xavier-hernandez/spanish-wordlist` — [GitHub](https://github.com/xavier-hernandez/spanish-wordlist); OpenSLR SLR21 frequency list from Spanish Gigaword — [OpenSLR](https://us.openslr.org/21); Sketch Engine frequency lists — [Sketch Engine](https://www.sketchengine.eu/spanish-word-list/)
- The scored lists constructors normally use are English: the Crossword Nexus Collaborative Word List (`word;score`) — [GitHub](https://github.com/Crossword-Nexus/collaborative-word-list)
- Academic: Esteche, Romero, Chiruzzo and Rosá (Universidad de la República, Uruguay) built a system generating Spanish crosswords from news text, definition extraction in Python and grid generation by a greedy strategy in Prolog (CLEI 2016; CLEI Electronic Journal 20(2), 2017). — [CLEI EJ](https://www.clei.org/cleiej/index.php/cleiej/article/view/25)

### Inferences
- A Spanish scored list would have to be built: a lexicon (hunspell with affixes expanded, or an RAE-derived list) joined to corpus frequency (OpenSLR/Gigaword) for scores. Licences of each source were not checked here.
- A greedy generator such as Esteche et al.'s almost certainly yields loosely crossed grids, not American ones (not verified against the paper's full text).

### Gaps
- No evidence of what tools elro or newspaper mini makers use. Nothing found on Spanish use of CrossFire, Qxw, Ingrid, Crosserville or Phil.

## 6. Other Romance languages

### Takeaway
French and Italian traditions also differ from the American format (French grids allow two-letter words), and recent academic work on Italian is about clue generation, not fully crossed fill.

### Cited Findings
- American grids are square with 180° rotational symmetry; francophone grids tolerate two-letter words, anglophone ones do not. — [Areq](https://areq.net/lang/fr/Mots_crois%C3%A9s.html)
- Universalis notes a grid using every cell exists as the "carré magique" (word square). — [Universalis](https://www.universalis.fr/encyclopedie/mots-croises/)
- Italian research: Zeinalipour et al. on Italian crossword generation with LLMs, and Zugarini et al.'s Clue-Instruct (LREC-COLING 2024, 44,075 examples, English) and ECWCA (CLiC-it 2024, Italian clue answering). — [Clue-Instruct](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.297/); [ECWCA](https://aclanthology.org/2024.clicit-1.138)

### Gaps
- Nothing found on Portuguese, or on French/Italian outlets running American-format minis.

## 7. Themed 15x15 puzzles with wordplay themers in Spanish

### Takeaway
Not confirmed. PasatiemposWeb says many of its "americanos" have a central theme, and elro's Crosshare work is the likeliest indie source, but I could not verify a single Spanish 15x15 with wordplay themers.

### Cited Findings
- PasatiemposWeb describes its clues as varied, with wordplay and cultural references, and says many crosswords have a central theme. — [PasatiemposWeb](https://www.pasatiemposweb.com/crucigrama_americano.php)
- Themed wordplay mixing the languages exists in English-language outlets (LA Times "Spanglish", 22 Sept 2024). — [LAXCrossword](https://laxcrossword.com/2024/09/22/)

### Gaps
- elro's puzzle sizes and themes could not be read (403). No named constructor of Spanish themed 15x15s was confirmed.
