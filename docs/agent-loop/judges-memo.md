Also: don't celebrate a best-of-one; show the time the player beat so the win reads earned.
- 2026-06-30 · F2 · For a viral share the headline metric must be a PERSONAL true brag for everyone who can share — a card that only celebrates the winner (third-person) gives non-winners nothing to post; threading the current user's rank turns every participant into a sharer, and that's the growth lever, not card polish. (Lost 2-1; flagged as the must-do follow-up on the winner.)
- 2026-07-01 · F2b · For a viral share the PUBLIC artifact's tone IS the product: a non-winner's caption/card must never wear a trophy — vary the emoji by outcome (win/tie/placement). - 2026-07-01 · F4 · For a viral challenge the MOMENTS are the product: a REAL challenger name (prompt when unset, warm fallback — never 'Player'), a compelling accept screen with a low-pressure 'just play' escape, and a NON-red graceful loss decide whether the friend accepts and re-shares — a perfectly correct one-tap flow that says 'Player challenges you' and paints the loss red spreads worse than a slightly heavier flow that feels personal and kind.
- 2026-07-01 · F4-live · For a growth-bridge CTA the label must match the MOMENT — 'Rematch Live' right after a ghost race converts far better than a generic 'Play Live', and a one-line hint that it's a FRESH live game (invite by link/QR) removes the 'do I re-race this / do I need a friend?' hesitation. - 2026-07-01 · F1-ui · For the cold-visitor activation lever the front-door CTA's VISUAL WEIGHT is the conversion — an eyebrow + gradient + larger type that reads as 'the thing to tap' beats a plain solid button of identical copy; and NEVER gate the instant-play CTA on auth-loading (a local daily briefly greyed out is a conversion leak at the worst instant).
- 2026-07-01 · F7 · For a first-visit nudge on an activation-focused front door, PLACEMENT beats polish — the orienting tip goes BELOW the hero CTA so it never pushes the primary action down or competes for the first tap; orientation is a bonus for readers, not a gate before the button.
