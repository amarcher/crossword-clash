This update (1.1.0, build 23) is the release candidate for the App Store. It fixes two things from build 22.

1. Finish a puzzle and tap Share. The share sheet now carries the whole result: the emoji grid, your time and the link. Build 22 sent only the link. Try Copy and Messages.
2. On an iPhone Duo's outer screen, the menu's Crossword Clash wordmark no longer runs under the clock. It fits beside the status bar.
3. Everything from build 22 still applies: built with Xcode 27.1 (full screen on iPhone Duo), public puzzle links after a publish, the profile menu above the studio grid, hardware keyboards, TV screens staying awake, Autocheck, community puzzles and the studio.
