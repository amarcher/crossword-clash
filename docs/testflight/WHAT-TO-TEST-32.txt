This build (1.1.0, build 32) corrects two lines of copy about notifications. Please check:

1. In a room's lobby, the hint under "Invite friends" now says that a friend whose app is closed gets a notification (if they allow them), and that otherwise the invite waits on their Race friends screen.
2. On your account page under Notifications, the "Room invites" switch now says it also covers a friend joining your room or waiting for you there.
3. Everything from build 31 still applies, and most of you never saw it, so please try it now: host a room, invite a friend, then lock your phone — their join arrives as "@name joined your room". Join a room whose host is away: their row shows Away, and "Nudge host" sends them "@name is waiting in your room" with a countdown before the next nudge. "Can't now" on an invite shows the host "Can't right now" in their lobby.
