This build (1.1.3, build 44) is about seeing how each game mode plays, and watching solves. Please check:

1. Game modes: in a room's lobby, the host's mode picker shows a small looping board for Compete, Co-op and Time trial. Players who join see a "Game mode" card with the mode the host picked.
2. Time trial: everyone solves their own copy, and each square another player has filled shows as a small dot in their colour. The standings show each player's percent filled until they finish.
3. Race replay: "You vs ..." now plays on one board. Your letters fill in shaded, and the other player's squares show as dots. It should fit the screen with nothing cut off.
4. Daily leaderboard: the Race button on a row looks like a button. Once you have solved that puzzle it becomes Watch, and plays that player's solve (Plus).
5. Notifications after signing in or out: if you allowed notifications before signing in to an existing account, invites and friend requests should now reach this device. After signing out, the account's notifications should stop.
6. Signing out: signing out on one device no longer signs your other devices out.
7. Sharing an older solve: reopen a puzzle you solved on another device (signed in) and tap Share. It should show the replay clip, not only the coloured squares.
8. Everything from build 42 still applies.
