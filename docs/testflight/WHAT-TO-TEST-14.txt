This update (1.1.0, build 14) answers the TestFlight feedback on builds 11 to 13.

1. Import from NYT: the Date field should now be a full-height field like the Puzzle one beside it, with the date readable inside it and nothing spilling past the edge. Pick a date, then Open.
2. Host a game from the app and have someone join from the website. Lock your phone for a minute, or switch apps. The other player's lobby should keep showing you the whole time (it used to drop you to a one-player list until your phone came back), and when you unlock, you should still be in the room with everyone listed.
3. On the website, opening the Join page should put the cursor in the game code box straight away.
4. On the Plus sheet, a subscription with no renewal date now says "Plus is active" instead of "Yours for good".
5. Sign in with Apple or Google still works (build 13's fix): the Plus sheet stays open and, if you bought Plus on an earlier build while signed out, Restore purchases brings it to your account.
