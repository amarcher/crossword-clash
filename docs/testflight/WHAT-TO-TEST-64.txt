1. Race friends, Start a room, then Change: under today's dailies there is "New to everyone here" with Mini, Midi and Large. It lists up to three past dailies that nobody in the room, or invited to it, has finished. Pick one and the room should return to the lobby with that puzzle.

6. Past leaderboards show "Back to today" with no sentence before it. Changing a room's puzzle has no sentence under the title. The Plus row for a subscriber reads "Subscriber".

8. Leaderboard, before you have solved that daily: tap Race beside a player. The puzzle should open at once on a card with that player ticked ("Race 1 solve" / "Go back"), with no page in between. Go back should return to the leaderboard in one tap.
9. Opening any fresh puzzle: the grid should be blurred behind the start card from the first moment, never readable first.
