6. Open a shared solve link: the challenge page has just the puzzle, the time, "Race …'s solve" and "Just solve it", with no paragraph under the button.
7. First open, signed out: no notification question. It comes after you sign in; the reminder question still comes after your first daily.
8. The finish screen no longer says "Your streak already counted today".
9. Everything from build 61 still applies.
