This build (1.1.1, build 36) is about typing, finished puzzles, leaderboards and friends. Please check:

1. Typing fast no longer refuses a correct letter. Type a word straight through a square that is already filled (from a crossing word, or another player's letter in a race): nothing shakes and nothing counts as wrong.
2. A wrong letter buzzes. Type a wrong letter with autocheck on and the phone vibrates. The switch "Vibrate on a wrong letter" sits beside Autocheck in the puzzle options and in the clue sheet; turn it off and the buzz stops.
3. The on-screen keys take fast two-thumb typing: press the next key before lifting from the last and both letters land.
4. A solved puzzle stays solved. Open a daily you finished: the grid is filled, the header says "You solved this", and only "Clear & redo" (with a confirmation) empties it. On the menu the solved daily's tile is tinted with a check and its button reads View.
5. Leaderboards: step back through past days with the arrows. If you solved that day's puzzle later, your time appears as "Not on the board" where it would have placed. With a friend on the board, the Everyone / Friends switch shows only you and your friends.
6. Friends: on Race friends → Friends, "Share my link" sends your add-me link and "Show QR code" shows it. Whoever opens the link sees an Add button. (On an iPhone the link opens the website for now.)
7. Plus: both plans now offer the first month free on the web. In the app the plans show "1 month free, then …" only once the store offers exist.
8. Everything from build 35 still applies.
