This build (1.1.2, build 40) adds a leaderboard for the Daily Plus puzzle and a few follow-ups. Please check:

1. Daily Plus: the extra Plus puzzle on the menu is now called "Daily Plus". Without Plus it shows locked, with its title, theme and grid shape under a padlock and "Unlock with Plus".
2. Daily Plus leaderboard: linked from the Daily Plus card, the archive's Daily Plus section and the finish screen. Everyone can see the times; a Plus member's finish on the day posts one. The arrows step back through past days.
3. Streak repair in the archive: the day after a single missed day, Past puzzles opens on the missed day with "Repair your streak" above its puzzles. With Plus, solving any of them that day keeps the streak.
4. See your stats: a button on the finish screen when you are signed in. Free accounts now see real figures on the stats page, with the Plus ones locked.
5. Solo play pauses after a minute without input, and the clock stops there.
6. Everything from build 39 still applies.
