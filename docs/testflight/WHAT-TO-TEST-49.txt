This build (1.1.3, build 49) is about seeing how each game mode plays, and watching solves. Please check:

1. Game modes: in a room's lobby, the host's mode picker shows a small looping board for Compete, Co-op and Time trial. Players who join see a "Game mode" card with the mode the host picked.
2. Time trial: everyone solves their own copy, and each square another player has filled shows a folded corner in their colour. The standings show each player's percent filled until they finish.
3. Race replay: "You vs ..." now plays on one board. Your letters fill in shaded, and the other player's squares show a folded corner, and the replay ends on the whole grid. It should fit the screen with nothing cut off.
4. Daily leaderboard: the Race button on a row looks like a button. Once you have solved that puzzle it becomes Watch, and plays that player's solve (Plus).
5. Notifications after signing in or out: if you allowed notifications before signing in to an existing account, invites and friend requests should now reach this device. After signing out, the account's notifications should stop.
6. Signing out: signing out on one device no longer signs your other devices out.
7. Sharing an older solve: reopen a puzzle you solved on another device (signed in) and tap Share. It should show the replay clip, not only the coloured squares.
8. Signed out means empty: sign out and the menu restarts with no streak, solved marks or puzzles in progress, and a "You're signed out" card. Sign back in and they return. (A phone that was already signed out before this build tidies itself after the next sign-in.)
9. Puzzles in progress follow any account, not only Plus: start a puzzle signed in, then open the same account on another device or the website and continue it.
10. Settings follow the account: Autocheck and Haptics, hidden community authors and the leaderboard's Everyone / Friends switch are the same on every device you sign in on.
11. Sign-in buttons: "Continue with Apple" and "Continue with Google" have the same text size, with the logos lined up.
12. Hosting a room: Game settings (the mode picker and its preview) sit right under the player list, and the little preview board has all four rounded corners.
13. Race buttons on the leaderboard: start the mini, switch to the midi, then finish both. Both finishes should be raceable by other players (each keeps its own recording now).
14. A puzzle across two devices: start a daily on one device, finish it on another signed in to the same account (both on this build). The finish should have a Race / Watch button on the leaderboard.
15. Daily cards on the menu: the label and the theme sit in a strip across the top of each card (white, and light blue once the puzzle is solved), and a long theme reads in full. Tapping anywhere on the Leaderboard strip opens the leaderboard.
16. Race share video: finish a Compete or Co-op race in a room. The finish screen should show a clip of the grid filling in each player's colour, and Share should send a link that plays it (no emoji squares). Try a room on a past daily or a community puzzle too.
17. Time trial share: finish a time trial; the finish screen should show the clip of your own solve.
18. Hosting a room: the Compete and Co-op preview fills words forwards, one player across and one down.
19. Everything from build 42 still applies.
