Welcome to the first Crossword Clash iPhone and iPad beta!

1. Play Today's Mini or choose a puzzle from the Classic library. Test tapping cells, switching Across/Down, keyboard entry, and clue navigation. Correct letters stay on the grid; incorrect letters are rejected.
2. Close and reopen the app during a solo puzzle. Confirm your letters and progress return.
3. With a friend on another device, create a room and join using its code. Start the game, claim letters, and check that both screens update. Try reopening the app during the game.
4. If you have access to NYT crosswords, open Import from NYT, choose a Daily or Mini edition, sign in on NYT, and tap Import this puzzle. Check the date, author, grid, and clues. Try a second import without signing in again.
5. Send screenshots and feedback through TestFlight, including your device model and puzzle date.

NYT import is optional. Included puzzles work without a NYT account. Rebus and other special-cell puzzles are not supported yet. Solo NYT imports stay on your device; hosting or sharing a challenge sends the puzzle to participants, as explained in the play picker.
