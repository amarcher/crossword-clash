This build (1.1.0, build 30) adds friend suggestions after a race. Please check:

1. Finish a race with someone who is signed in but not yet your friend: the finish screen shows "Raced with you" with an Add friend button beside their name, which turns to Requested (or Friends, if they had already asked you).
2. The rows never appear for spectators, for players without an account, for someone already your friend, or with no other accounts in the room.
3. Everything from build 29 still applies: the regrouped menu with one-line rows, the Plus cards on Past puzzles and Community puzzles, and friend requests answerable from the menu, the account menu and the account page.
