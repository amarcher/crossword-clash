Crossword Clash turns crosswords into a game you can play together. Solve the daily mini, explore the classic puzzle library, import your own puzzle files, or host a live room and invite friends with a code. Solo progress stays available when you reopen the app.

This first iPhone and iPad beta also includes an optional NYT importer: choose a Daily or Mini edition, sign in on NYT with your own access, and tap Import. Crossword Clash is an independent app and is not affiliated with The New York Times. A NYT subscription is not needed for the included puzzles or core game.

Please test puzzle entry, keyboard behavior, saved progress after reopening, and live rooms with another device. NYT import currently supports standard single-letter puzzles; rebus and other special cells are not supported.
