This build (1.1.4, build 58) is a trial of one way into a puzzle. Please check:

1. One Play button: on the home screen each daily has a single "Play" button. The "Race friends" button beside it is gone. The theme is a gold badge again and the line under "DAILY MINI" is gone.
2. How do you want to play? Tap "Play" on a puzzle you haven't started. Before any countdown, a card over the blurred grid asks how you want to play: "Play solo", "Race a finished solve" (when a friend or anyone else has a solve to race) and "Race friends live".
3. Play solo: counts 3, 2, 1 and starts. The countdown should always be the last thing before the grid.
4. Race a finished solve: opens a list with times. Tick up to three and tap "Race 1 solve". The countdown says who you are racing, and their squares fill beside yours from the same start. This no longer needs Plus: please try it on an account without Plus.
5. Race friends live: creates a room straight away under your username and opens the lobby. Invite friends from the lobby's list (friends who already solved the puzzle have a "Solved" chip) or share the room code. While you are alone in the room, "Play solo now" closes it and takes you to the countdown.
6. Leaderboard rows: "Race" and "Watch" on a regular daily's leaderboard no longer need Plus. The Daily Plus is unchanged.
7. Race friends screen (from the home screen): with a puzzle picked, a friend who has already solved it shows "Watch" instead of "Challenge". If you have solved it and they haven't, their row shows "Send time".
8. Everything from build 57 still applies.
