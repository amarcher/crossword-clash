Race a finished solve: tap Play on a daily and pick whose solves to race. Their squares fill in on your grid as you go, on your clock.

One Play button asks how you want to play, and every puzzle starts behind a 3-2-1 countdown.

Sharing sends a link whose preview plays your solve. You choose who sees your solves: everyone, friends or nobody.

Rooms suggest past dailies nobody there has finished. Leaderboards have tabs for each daily and count your first solve.
