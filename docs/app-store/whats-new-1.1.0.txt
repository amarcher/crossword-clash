Crossword Clash Plus: an optional subscription with the full daily archive, the crossword construction studio (with AI help for clues and titles), progress synced across your devices and more narrator time on the TV view.

Sign in with Apple or Google to keep your username, solve history and streaks on every device.

Community puzzles: play crosswords made by other solvers, and publish your own from the studio.

Autocheck setting: leave it on to have wrong letters refused, or turn it off to type freely and check when the grid is full.

Also: the app works with a hardware keyboard, the TV view keeps the screen awake, you're told when an update is available, and the share sheet after a finish carries your emoji grid and time.
