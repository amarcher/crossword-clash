No login is required to play. The app creates an anonymous session automatically; signing in and the subscription are optional.

Core path: on the menu tap Play under Daily Mini for a built-in crossword. Tap a cell and type with the crossword keyboard. With Autocheck on (the default), correct letters stay on the grid and incorrect letters are rejected; with it off (the switch under the scoreboard in the clue sheet), any letter can be typed and the grid can be checked when it is full. Close and reopen the app to see saved solo progress.

Multiplayer (needs internet and two devices, or one device plus a browser): choose Start a game, pick a built-in puzzle, enter a display name and create the room. On a second device open Join with a code (or visit crosswordclash.com and choose Join) and enter the six-character room code. The host can start once two players are in.

Accounts (optional): Sign in with Apple or Google, from "Sign in" at the top of the menu. An account keeps the player's username, solve history and streaks on every device. Any Apple ID or Google account works; no test account is needed. Account deletion is in the app (Your account > Delete account) and at crosswordclash.com/support.

Crossword Clash Plus (auto-renewable subscription, plus_monthly $3.99 and plus_annual $24.99): open it from the "Crossword Clash Plus" row on the menu or from Your account > About Plus. The paywall lists the benefits (the full daily archive, the crossword construction studio with AI clue drafting, progress synced across devices, more AI narrator time on the TV view) and the two plans with their prices, "Restore purchases", and links to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. The app asks the player to sign in before buying so the subscription follows their account. Purchases go through StoreKit; nothing in the app links to a web checkout.

Community puzzles and the studio (user-generated content): Plus subscribers can build a crossword in the studio and publish it under their username; anyone can play published puzzles from "Community puzzles" on the menu. AI clue drafting only drafts text the author asked for, and the author edits it before publishing; nothing reaches other players until the author publishes. Every published puzzle has a Report option (Copied / Offensive / Nonsense / Something else) and "Hide this author"; each report is emailed to the developer, three reporters in a week hold a puzzle automatically, and the developer can remove puzzles and block authors. Publishing requires accepting the community rules in the Terms of Use. Display names and usernames are filtered for objectionable words and can be reported through the Support page linked from the menu footer.

Optional import: Import from NYT opens nytimes.com in a separate in-app browser. The person signs in on NYT's own page (use the menu at the top left of the NYT page, then Log In). When they tap Import, the app first asks NYT whether this visitor is signed in with an active subscription that includes the crossword, and refuses with a "Please log in to NYT first" message if not. Only then does it read the puzzle the person has open. The app bundles no NYT content or credentials, does not see the sign-in, and keeps solo imports on the device; hosting a room sends a copy of the puzzle to the other players, as the app explains before doing so. Crossword Clash is not affiliated with The New York Times. Review can be completed entirely with the built-in puzzles, without an NYT account.

The app contains no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs and no tracking. It sends first-party play statistics (puzzle progress, time spent, per-clue timings) to the developer's own backend, as the privacy policy describes.
