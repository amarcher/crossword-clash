Race a finished solve. Tap Play on a daily and pick whose solves to race. Their squares fill in on your grid as you go, on your clock, so a friend who solved this morning can still be raced tonight.

One Play button. It asks how you want to play: on your own, against finished solves, or in a room with friends.

Every puzzle starts behind a 3-2-1 countdown, so the clock never runs before you can see the grid.

Sharing sends a link, and the link's preview plays your solve.

You choose who sees your solves: everyone, friends or nobody.

Rooms suggest past dailies that nobody in the room has finished.

Leaderboards have tabs for the Mini, Midi, Large and Daily Plus, and count your first solve of each puzzle.
