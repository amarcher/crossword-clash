Crossword Clash turns the crossword into something you play together.

Start a room, share a six-character code, and race your friends on the same grid in real time. Every correct letter you place is yours, shown in your color, and the scores update as you go. Prefer teamwork? Switch to co-op and finish the puzzle together, or pick time trial so everyone solves their own copy against the clock.

PLAY TOGETHER
• Live rooms for two or more players. Join with a code or a QR scan
• Three modes: Compete, Co-op and Time trial
• Friends can join from any phone, tablet or computer at crosswordclash.com, with no download and no account
• Put the board on a TV for game night while everyone plays from their phone
• Optional wrong-answer penalty to keep the guessers honest

SOLVE ON YOUR OWN
• Three fresh crosswords every day: the Mini, the Midi and the Large, each with streaks and a daily leaderboard
• A calendar of every past daily to catch up on
• Puzzles made by other solvers in the community library
• Open your own .puz, .ipuz, .jpz and .xd puzzle files
• Autocheck on or off: have wrong letters refused, or type freely and check at the end
• Your progress is saved automatically

MADE FOR TOUCH
• A crossword keyboard built for fast entry, with the active clue always in view
• iPhone and iPad, portrait or landscape
• English and Spanish

Free to play, with no ads. Sign in with Apple or Google (optional) to keep your username, solve history and streaks on every device.

CROSSWORD CLASH PLUS (optional subscription)
• The full daily archive, back to the first day
• The construction studio: build your own crossword with a word-fill engine and AI help for clues and titles, then publish it for everyone
• Progress synced across your devices
• More narrator time on the TV view
