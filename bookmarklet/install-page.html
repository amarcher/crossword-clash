NYT → Crossword Clash Bookmarklet

A one-click bookmarklet that imports the New York Times crossword you're currently viewing into Crossword Clash so you can play it head-to-head with friends.

You're on a small screen. Bookmarklets need a desktop browser with a visible bookmarks bar (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge on a computer). Open this page on a computer to install it — it won't work on a phone or tablet.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Show your bookmarks bar

    If you don't see one under the address bar, press Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows/Linux) to show it.

  2. Drag the button below into your bookmarks bar

    Drag it — don't click it. Clicking it here won't do anything useful; it only works once it's a bookmark and you click it from a NYT crossword page.

    NYT → Crossword Clash ← drag this up ↑

  3. Open a NYT puzzle, then click the bookmark

    Visit any Daily, Mini, or other NYT crossword while logged into your subscription. Click the bookmark you just added — a new tab opens showing "Receiving puzzle...", then the puzzle is ready to play.

What this does: When clicked on a NYT crossword page, the bookmarklet fetches the puzzle from the NYT crossword API using your existing subscription cookie, converts it to Crossword Clash's format, and opens a new tab at crosswordclash.com with the puzzle loaded. It does not send your credentials anywhere else and requires an active NYT Crossword subscription. Full source is available on GitHub.

How it works

  • Reads puzzle data from nytimes.com/svc/crosswords/v6/puzzle/... using the session cookie already in your browser.
  • Converts the puzzle into Crossword Clash's internal format and compresses it with lz-string.
  • Opens a new tab to crosswordclash.com and transfers the puzzle via window.postMessage (with a clipboard copy as a backup, in case the automatic transfer is blocked).
  • No data is sent to any third-party server — the transfer is entirely between your two browser tabs.

Trouble installing? Some browsers require you to right-click the button and choose "Bookmark this link" instead of dragging — try that if the drag doesn't take.
